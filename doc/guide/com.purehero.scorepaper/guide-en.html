Your first score

ScorePaper recognizes notes and rhythm in photos of printed sheet music and lets you edit the result. Recognition runs on your device and works without an internet connection.

  1. On Home, choose Photograph a score. If you already have a photo, choose Import a photo.
  2. Align the four corners with the score and apply the correction.
  3. Tap Recognize score to load an editable score.
  4. Compare the result with the original and correct notes and rhythm as needed.
  5. Choose Export to save PDF or PNG.
Your current draft is saved automatically on this device. Starting a new score replaces the current draft. Export work you want to keep first. An exported file is not an editable project backup.

Capture and load sheet music

Take a clear photo

  • Lay the page as flat as possible and use good lighting.
  • Keep the camera parallel to the page and hold it steady.
  • Keep fingers, shadows and reflections away from the notes.
  • Include all the staves and notes you want to recognize. If the notes are too small, move closer or capture smaller sections.

Photograph a score opens your device’s camera app. Take a photo and confirm that you want to use it to return to ScorePaper. If no camera app is available, choose Import a photo and select an image file instead.

Align the corners and recognize

Position the four corners around the score. You can also use the corner selector and arrow buttons when dragging is difficult. Keep the corners from crossing, then apply the correction.

On the photo review screen, tap Recognize score and wait for processing to finish. Use Adjust corners to revise the selected area.

Results depend on photo quality, typography and musical complexity. Notes, lyrics and expression markings can be misread. Compare with the original before performing or printing.

Compare with the original

Use Compare in the editor to check the photo against the editable score. Portrait mode stacks them vertically; landscape places them side by side. Pinch to zoom the source photo and drag to pan.

  • Confirmed: mark an item as reviewed.
  • Later: leave it for another look.
  • Close comparison: return to normal editing.

These labels record your review decisions, not recognition accuracy scores. You can recognize the photo again from the More menu, but recognizing again replaces current edits with the new recognition result.

Select and edit notes

Tap a measure to enlarge it, then tap a note to select it. The current measure is blue and the selected note has an orange border. You can select notes directly on different staves. Check the selection details for the measure, staff and voice.

Move notes OFF is the default mode.

  • Longer / Shorter: increase or decrease duration.
  • Center duration button: choose directly from a whole note to a 64th note.
  • Previous note / Next note: select a note on the same staff and in the same voice.
  • Previous measure / Next measure: move to an adjacent measure.

Use ＋ Add to add notes or rests, or Symbols to select a symbol from a list. Use Undo to reverse an edit and Redo to apply it again. Full score returns to the page view.

Change pitch and beat position

Turn on Move notes to change what the arrows do.

  • Higher / Lower: change the pitch by one diatonic step on the same staff. Use ♯, ♭ and ♮ for chromatic changes.
  • Left / Right: move the selected note itself earlier or later. Choose an eighth-note or sixteenth-note movement step.

The note keeps its duration. Rests fill the space it leaves, and the destination must have enough space occupied by rests. Other notes are not shifted or swapped.

You cannot move across a measure boundary or overlap another note. Tuplets, grace notes, connections and beam-group boundaries can also restrict movement. Rests cannot change pitch or beat position in movement mode. Read the explanation shown in the app.

Chords, beams and lyrics

Deletion and chords

Deleting a note replaces it with an equal-length rest. In a chord, you can remove the selected note or replace the entire chord with a rest. Check whether an individual note or the whole chord is selected. Duration changes affect the whole chord. Deleting a rest also removes its duration and shifts following notes earlier.

Beam notes

Select the first note → Beam notes → select the last note → Apply beam. You can join consecutive eighth notes or shorter notes in the same measure, staff and voice. Select a beamed note to see Unbeam. New beams have restrictions around rests, longer notes and tuplet boundaries.

Lyrics and connections

Select a note and use Enter lyrics, then save and continue to the next note. Choose from five Lyric size settings; the setting also applies to exports. To add a connection, select its start and end notes and apply it.

Read your score or start a new one

  • Score only hides editing tools. Tap Edit to return.
  • Pinch to zoom, or tap a measure to enlarge it.
  • Use More (⋮) → Choose page to change pages. Page arrows are available in reading mode.
  • Rotate to landscape for one continuous horizontal score and swipe sideways to read it. Portrait restores the page view.
  • To compose without a photo, choose Start with a blank staff on Home. Export your current work first if you want to keep it.

Save PDF or PNG

  1. Tap Export at the top of the editor.
  2. Select Include title if you want a title. You can rename the score by tapping its title in the editor.
  3. Choose PDF for all pages or PNG for the current page.
  4. In the system file picker, check the folder and file name and finish saving.

The title appears on the first PDF page or above the PNG image. Your lyric-size setting is included. Note-selection highlights are not exported.

The saved file contains the edited score, not a PDF copy of the original photo. Find the exported file in your file manager and share it when needed.

Troubleshooting

Notes are missing or recognized incorrectly.

Try a brighter, sharper photo and check that no staves are cut off. Capture smaller sections of wide or complex music. Compare the recognized result with the original and correct it in the editor.

A note will not move where I want.

Check that Move notes is enabled and there are enough empty beats in the same measure. Overlapping notes and complex connections can prevent movement.

Saving or exporting does not work.

Wait for score loading and edit saving to finish. Choose another writable folder in the file picker and check the device’s available storage.

How do I return to an earlier score?

Continue editing on Home opens the current draft. This is not a library of multiple projects: export anything you need before starting a new score. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can remove the local draft.

Do I need the internet?

Music and text recognition models are bundled and work offline. Opening this online guide, sending email and updating through Google Play may require a connection.

Language, updates and support

The interface follows your device or app language and supports Korean and English. Other languages use English. Titles and lyrics you enter are not translated.

An important Google Play update may open an update prompt. If you cancel a required update, you must retry or exit. Automatic checks run on Home after saving finishes. Availability may be limited offline or when installed outside Google Play.

Choose Report a problem on Home or in the score menu. Describe the issue, review the report, and send it from your email app. The report includes the app version, device model and Android version. Photos and scores are not attached automatically.
